## Looking Towards the Future

Science continually opens new questions and possibilities. Advances in biomedical research, stem-cell technology, optogenetics, and drug discovery offer avenues for investigating complex vision disorders.

For Muhanad, these developments inspire an important question:

**How can scientific discovery contribute to future possibilities for vision restoration?**

This is a question for careful research, open scientific discussion, and collaboration. Promising research approaches must be evaluated rigorously; they should not be mistaken for established treatments.
